**DineOps — For Restaurant Owners**

- **What this app does for you:**
  - Manage your restaurant profile, operating hours, and settings.
  - Build and maintain your menu (categories, items, prices, images).
  - Receive, track, and update orders (table, zone, and take-away flows).
  - View basic reports (sales, wastage, inventory alerts) and export data.

- **Typical workflows:**
  - Onboarding: create account → add restaurant details → set operating hours and menu.
  - Orders: view incoming orders in the dashboard, change status (Preparing → Ready → Delivered), notify customers.
  - Menu management: add/edit items, toggle availability, set zone-specific prices.

- **Tips & best practices:**
  - Keep menu item names and photos clear — customers convert better with good images.
  - Use inventory thresholds to avoid out-of-stock surprises.
  - Regularly check `Reports` for wastage and cost trends.

- **Where to go in the UI:**
  - Dashboard: live orders and quick stats.
  - Menu: categories and items editor.
  - Settings: payment providers, staff accounts, notifications.

- **Need help?** Contact your platform administrator or open an issue in the repo describing the problem and steps to reproduce.
